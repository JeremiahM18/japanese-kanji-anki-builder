# v0.3.0-beta.5 N5 Automation-Reviewed Preview

## Release Decision

`v0.3.0-beta.5` is the current published, exact **automation-reviewed preview**.
It is not production/GA and is not human/device-QA approved. The published
`v0.3.0-beta.4` release remains an immutable predecessor; beta.5 was required
because governed code, security controls, and release evidence changed after
that tag.

Required public label:

> AUTOMATION-REVIEWED PREVIEW - HUMAN DEVICE QA NOT PERFORMED

The repository owner accepted the bounded residual risk in `PROD-REL-001` on
2026-08-01 because independent human QA, physical mobile-device QA, interactive
screen-reader QA, listening/naturalness review, and stroke-sequence visual
review are not realistically available for this release horizon. That
constraint must remain visible; automation evidence must never be renamed as
human evidence.

## Exact Scope

Included:

- core kanji deck, JLPT N5 only: exactly `80` notes and `80` cards
- core word deck, JLPT N5 only: exactly `588` `written|reading` notes and cards
- the exact two APKG assets named and hashed in the version-3 release QA packet
- the packet, CycloneDX SBOM, dependency-license inventory, checksum manifest,
  verification-material archive, provenance attestations, and SBOM attestations

Excluded:

- N4, N3, N2, and N1 core kanji decks
- N4, N3, N2, and N1 word decks
- additional-unverified kanji decks
- broader historic locks or review-lane work outside the two shipped APKGs
- every production/GA, human-approved, device-approved, or
  accessibility-conformance claim

The final protected-main commit, isolated candidate run ID, paths, names,
counts, byte sizes, and SHA-256 values are release-time packet evidence. Word
notes use exact `written|reading` identity. Earlier locally generated word
APKGs may have used written-only GUIDs; use a fresh profile or remove the
earlier test deck rather than assuming an in-place identity migration.

## Governed Changes Since beta.4

- The N5 reading-gap plan now records `99` exact governed dispositions for `83`
  missing reading targets and `16` variant gaps. Those are adjudication records,
  not `99` new cards; no unsupported card was added to inflate coverage.
- N5 readiness fails closed when reading-override inputs drift, and regression
  coverage proves the orchestrator forwards its required scope argument.
- The forced patched Sharp dependency is guarded by a fail-closed Transformers
  compatibility policy that restricts executable imports and pipeline/model
  tasks to the validated text-embedding surface. It does not certify image or
  vision compatibility.
- Reviewed dependency-license exceptions are bound to exact package versions,
  and the due hosted, local-runtime, hostile-input, source-governance, and NLP
  risk evidence was renewed from current commands.

These changes do not expand the release scope, transfer authority between
Silver, Gold, Sapphire, Platinum, and Obsidian, or convert source/NLP evidence
into card certification.

## Automated Assurance Required

Publication requires all applicable controls on the exact release commit and
downloaded draft assets, including:

- full local-data N5 readiness bound into the packet
- N5 Silver, Gold, Sapphire, Platinum, and Obsidian readiness for both deck kinds
- clean-hosted tracked-only N5 contract, artifact, placement, and policy checks
- exact version, tag, commit, deck scope, asset name, size, SHA-256, and count binding
- APKG ZIP, media-map, SQLite, collection-version, deck, field, GUID, card, and
  media-reference inspection
- all `80` packaged kanji notes and all `588` exact `written|reading` word notes
  checked against tracked full-level Golden manifests, with no unmatched rows
- managed-media provenance and integrity gates
- exact-candidate static accessibility review
- lint, typecheck, tests, documentation/lane audits, supply-chain, advisory,
  license, secret, SBOM, branch-protection, security-requirements,
  pre-release-trust, CI-smoke, and release gates
- protected-main CI and CodeQL before tagging
- tagged-workflow checksums, SBOM, provenance/SBOM attestations, constrained
  `gh attestation verify` for every published asset, and an immutable Actions
  evidence artifact that explicitly includes the hidden `.release-bundle`

The hosted tracked-only readiness mode never reads ignored workstation data and
does not claim local media provenance or regenerated local Gold. Those
authorities remain in the commit-bound packet and exact APKG Golden inspection.

## Explicit Accepted Limitations

- desktop-anki-import-not-performed
- mobile-qa-not-performed
- screen-reader-interaction-not-performed
- listening-naturalness-not-performed
- stroke-sequence-visual-review-not-performed

Static, structural, provenance, and packaged-field automation reduces risk but
does not prove those interactions or perceptions. A future production/GA
release still requires actual passed evidence for them unless governance is
changed through a separately reviewed contract and risk decision.
